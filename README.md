# Pin This!

Pins your most important sheets to the top of the screen so you're always one click away from them. Works for the Game Master and players alike.

It works a lot like the macro hotbar, but instead of running a macro, it opens the sheet itself, just like clicking it in its usual tab.

## About

This module adds a shelf at the top of your screen. Drag anything onto it, an actor, a journal (or a page of the journal), a scene, and it shows up as a small tab. Click that tab any time to open the sheet again, no matter which sidebar tab it's normally filed under.

It works a bit like the macro hotbar at the bottom of your screen, but instead of a macro, you're pinning the sheet to be viewed or dragged into the scene. It's also harder to lose by accident: unpinning is its own separate button, so you won't bump it the way you might accidentally delete a macro slot.

## Why?

For Game Masters, you spend less time digging through tabs for the sheet you need. You lose fewer things by accident, like closing a sheet with Escape or the X button and then having to go find it again. And you spend less time setting the same things up every session, because everything important is already pinned right where you want it.

For players, pin the things you reach for most, maybe your character sheet, your session journal, or a compendium full of items. That way you can grab what you need in one click instead of digging through the Actors or Journal tabs, and spend more time actually chatting and roleplaying.

## List of Pinnable Sheets:

Open the context menu then click "📌 Toggle Pin", click the 📌 button in the sheet's header, or drag any of these onto the shelf:

- Actors
- Items (weapons, gear, spells, etc)
- Journal entries, and individual pages inside a journal
- Scenes
- Roll tables
- Macros
- Playlists
- Card decks
- Whole compendium packs

## Limitations

- Pinning works by dragging something onto the shelf, so if it can't normally be dragged in Foundry, it can't be pinned either. A few premium modules build their own custom "contents" pages out of plain clickable text instead of real draggable links, so those can't be dragged straight onto the shelf. Open the page itself instead and drag it in from the sidebar.
- Pinning a journal page always opens it at the top of the page instead of at a specific heading inside it.
- You can pin a whole compendium pack but not a single folder inside it.
